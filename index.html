Where the word came from

Jackson Palmer, co-founder of Dogecoin and the originator of the idea, first conceived of it after noticing the constant stream of new altcoins flooding the market — what we now call memecoins.

As he later explained:

Dogecoin also gained its developer in Billy Markus — better known as Shibetoshi Nakamoto — who became a co-founder after joining forces with Jackson Palmer. Building on Palmer’s original idea, Markus stepped in as the project’s lead developer and helped bring Dogecoin to life.

One of them named the joke. The other built it. What neither of them ever claimed was the word itself — something. It sat there for a decade, the placeholder for every coin that ever launched on a whim. Now it has a ticker.
